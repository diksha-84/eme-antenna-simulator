📘 THEORY & FORMULAS USED

Wavelengthλ = c / f , k = 2π/λWave solution of Maxwell's equations in free space, c = 3×10⁸ m/s
Far-field of finite dipoleEθ ∝ [cos(kL/2·cosθ) − cos(kL/2)] / sinθFrom the sinusoidal current I(z)=I₀ sin[k(L/2−|z|)]. For L=λ/2: cos(π/2·cosθ)/sinθ
Power density & radiation intensityU = r²|E|²/(2η₀) , η₀ = 120π ΩPoynting vector → radiation intensity U(θ)
DirectivityD = 4πUmax/Prad = 2F²max / ∫F² sinθ dθHalf-wave dipole: D = 1.64 (2.15 dBi)
Radiation resistanceRr = (η₀/2π)∫F² sinθ dθ / sin²(kL/2)Half-wave dipole: ≈ 73 Ω
Ohmic loss & efficiencyRs=√(πfμ₀/σ) , RL=RsL/(4πa) , e=Rr/(Rr+RL)Skin-effect surface resistance of the conductor
GainG = e · DGain = directivity × radiation efficiency
Reflection, S11, VSWRΓ=(Zin−Z₀)/(Zin+Z₀) , S11=20log|Γ| , RL=−S11 , VSWR=(1+|Γ|)/(1−|Γ|)Zin = Rr+RL+jX (X from Si/Ci integrals; X≈+42.5 Ω at exactly λ/2)
